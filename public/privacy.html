01

About Relay

Relay is used by Oaknet staff and approved contractors to manage dispatches, site work, the vehicle fleet, movement plans, claims and approvals. It is not offered to the public.

02

Information we hold

  • Account detailsName, work email address, role and, if provided, a WhatsApp phone number.
  • Work recordsDispatches, deliveries, movement plans, vehicle checks, fuel logs, claims and approvals made in Relay.
  • Supporting documentsReceipts attached to claims, and vehicle and driver documents such as insurance and inspection certificates and driving licences.
We do not keep copies of vehicle logbooks, national ID cards or passports in Relay.
03

How we use it

Only to run our operations: sending work notifications and approval requests, recording decisions, reimbursing claims and keeping vehicle and driver documents up to date.

We do not use it for marketing and we do not sell it.

04

Notifications: app, email and WhatsApp

Notifications appear in Relay and are emailed to the staff member's work address. If their account has a WhatsApp number, they are also sent to it through the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta, and approvers can approve or reject a request from WhatsApp. We only send messages related to the person's work in Relay.

So that a notification doesn't stay unread after it has been seen, Relay records when it is read in the app, when WhatsApp reports it as read, and when its email is opened (through a small image or the "Open in Relay" link in the email). This is used only to mark the notification as read.

Anyone can stop receiving WhatsApp messages by removing their number on the Relay Notifications page, or by contacting us.

05

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run Relay for us, and only as needed:

Meta Platforms · WhatsAppHostinger · hostingMongoDB Atlas · databaseEmail provider · notifications & account emails

We disclose information to authorities only where the law requires it.

06

How long we keep it

For as long as it is needed for our operations and for legal, tax and audit requirements, after which it is deleted. When a document is renewed, the earlier version may be kept as a record.

07

Security

Access is limited by role, sign-in requires two-factor authentication, and data is sent over encrypted connections.

08

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it. Contact us using the details below.
