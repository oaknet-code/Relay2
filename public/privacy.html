Relay Privacy Policy

Oaknet Business Limited

Last updated: 5 November 2026

About Relay

Relay is Oaknet Business Limited's internal operations system. Our staff and approved contractors use it to manage dispatches, site work, the vehicle fleet, movement plans, claims and approvals. It is not offered to the public.

Information we hold

  • Account details: name, work email address, role and, if provided, a WhatsApp phone number.
  • Work records: dispatches, deliveries, movement plans, vehicle checks, fuel logs, claims and approvals made in Relay.
  • Supporting documents: receipts attached to claims, and vehicle and driver documents such as insurance and inspection certificates and driving licences.

We do not keep copies of vehicle logbooks, national ID cards or passports in Relay.

How we use it

Only to run our operations: sending work notifications and approval requests, recording decisions, reimbursing claims and keeping vehicle and driver documents up to date. We do not use it for marketing and we do not sell it.

WhatsApp messages

If a staff member's account has a WhatsApp number, Relay sends their work notifications and approval requests to it through the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta, and approvers can reply from WhatsApp to approve or reject a request. We only send messages related to the person's work in Relay. Anyone can stop receiving WhatsApp messages by removing their number on the Relay Notifications page, or by contacting us.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run Relay for us, and only as needed: Meta Platforms (WhatsApp messaging), Hostinger (hosting), MongoDB Atlas (database) and our email provider (account emails such as password resets). We disclose information to authorities only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

For as long as it is needed for our operations and for legal, tax and audit requirements, after which it is deleted. When a document is renewed, the earlier version may be kept as a record.

Security

Access is limited by role, sign-in requires two-factor authentication, and data is sent over encrypted connections.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it, by contacting us below.
